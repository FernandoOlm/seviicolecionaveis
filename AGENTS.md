Checkout pickup validation lives in browser-safe helpers under src/lib, with focused tests; event pickup uses shippingMethod arrange with schedule_kind 'event', orders.event_id linking the events table, and the event name persisted in order notes, keeping all payment paths compatible.
Pickup events (Retirada em eventos) live in the events table with rules in src/lib/events.ts (1h slots, per-day closing 24h before start, reopen at 10h the day after the event ends); admin CRUD is at /admin/eventos, the client calendar is EventSlotPicker, and reopening runs in the same pickup-reopen cron hook.
Schedule confirmations run in the paid-order pipeline with separate per-order client/store idempotency keys, reuse the existing email sender and app_settings recipient, and generate Google Calendar links in a pure tested helper; no OAuth or email queue is needed.
Regular-order statuses, labels, and paid-stage classification live in the browser-safe order-status module; reuse them across admin, customer, email, payment, and stock flows to prevent lifecycle inconsistencies. Service orders retain a separate lifecycle.- Scheduling availability (24h lead, America/Fortaleza) and pickup reopen rules live in src/lib/scheduling.ts and are enforced both in the picker and server-side; reopened pickups are flagged on the order (history kept, card stack deadlines untouched) by a daily cron right after the 14h deadline.
